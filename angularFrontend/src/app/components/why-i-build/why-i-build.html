Why I built BTG Spanish

I took Spanish throughout elementary school, high school, and even college. But when I found myself living with a host family in the middle of a pueblo in Oaxaca, Mexico — I could not communicate at all.

Thank goodness I was there to attend an immersive Spanish school, where I learned to communicate by practicing in real time with native speakers every single day for eight months.

Years after I moved back home, I noticed something unsettling. I was starting to get nervous when I spoke Spanish. Forgetting everything the moment I randomly had an opportunity to actually use it.

And I realized that the difference between my Mexico self and my "back from Mexico" self wasn't location...it was the time spent in actual, real conversations. So I built the bridge from knowing Spanish to speaking it.

BTG Spanish has grown into a robust program supporting adult Spanish learners at every stage, from zero to advanced. And the best part? I get to witness the full circle moment that so many of them experience as they step into the confident Spanish they've always dreamed of having.
